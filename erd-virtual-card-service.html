💰 Monthly Fee Billing Flow
1
SUBSCRIPTION active → generates a MONTHLY_FEE_CHARGE each billing cycle.
2
MONTHLY_FEE_CHARGE → amount debited from the customer's VIRTUAL_CARD.
3
PAYMENT → records the settlement from the linked ACCOUNT.
4
NOTIFICATION → alerts the CUSTOMER before & after each charge.
